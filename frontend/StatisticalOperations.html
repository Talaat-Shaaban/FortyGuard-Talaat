STATISTICAL ENGINE — n8n WORKFLOW

Statistical Operations in Heat Stress Analysis

Six sequential statistical operations converting real-time temperature readings into reliable predictive reports for every farmer, free of misleading numbers.
RISK LEVEL
Moving Average
mean = Σ(temps) / 7
Smoothes out single-day fluctuations to establish a solid baseline for the past week's temperature.
Standard Deviation
σ = √( Σ(t − mean)² / n )
Measures weather variance around the mean — forming the foundation for all subsequent calculations.
Z-Score
Z = (t − mean) / σ
Detects whether the daily temperature is an anomaly and tracks sudden heatwaves.
Cumulative Normal Distribution
P(risk) = 1 − CDF(Z_threshold)
The core engine: converts distance from critical thresholds into real probabilities (not misleading raw percentages) of heat stress within 48 hours.
Cumulative Stress Units
GDD = Σ max(t − threshold, 0)
An established agricultural standard (Growing Degree Days) measuring accumulated damage over time, not just single moments.
Trend Analysis
trend = t − mean ± 1.5°C
Quick weather state classification: stable, rapidly rising, moderately rising, or dropping.
Applied to: maize · dairy cows · beef cattle · tomatoes · soybeans · lettuce · strawberries
