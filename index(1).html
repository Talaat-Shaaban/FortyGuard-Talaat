📊 Z-Score Deviation Index

Measures how severely current local thermal conditions deviate from the historical 7-day baseline average:

Z = (X - μ) / σ

A score exceeding +1.0 indicates a sudden, critical thermal shock requiring immediate intervention.

🔥 Cumulative Thermal Stress

Calculated in °C-days above critical physiological thresholds. Prolonged heat build-up suppresses immune function and drastically reduces yield efficiency over time.
